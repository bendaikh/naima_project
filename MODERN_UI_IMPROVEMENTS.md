## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
- **Primary**: Blue (500-600) → Indigo (500-600)
- **Secondary**: Slate (50-900)
- **Accent Colors**:
  - Success: Emerald/Green
  - Warning: Amber/Orange
  - Info: Purple/Violet

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Instrument Sans + Inter
- **Weights**: 300-800
- **Sizes**: Responsive scaling (text-xs to text-4xl)

### Shadows
- `shadow-lg`: Standard elevation
- `shadow-xl`: Hover states
- `shadow-2xl`: High emphasis

### Border Radius
- `rounded-2xl`: Standard cards
- `rounded-3xl`: Large containers
- `rounded-full`: Circular elements

### Animations
- **Hover Scale**: 1.02-1.1 transform
- **Fade In Up**: Entry animations
- **Transitions**: 200-300ms duration
- **Easing**: ease-out
